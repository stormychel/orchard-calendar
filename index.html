Orchard Calendar

Your iCloud calendars, at home on Android.

Get it on Google Play

A calendar, not a sync setting

Switched to Android but your calendars still live in iCloud? Sign in once and every calendar you choose is there: work, home, family, shared calendars and birthdays, in their own colours, kept up to date in the background. Month, day and year views, an agenda list and search.

Private by design

Orchard Calendar talks directly to iCloud. There is no Orchard server in between and no account to create. Your password is encrypted on your phone. No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Privacy policy.

Free to view, one purchase to edit

Viewing and searching is free. Orchard Pro, a one-time purchase with no subscription, lets you create, edit and delete events and add more than one account.

Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

Orchard Calendar is an Android app by Michel Storms ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your calendar and your password stay between your phone and your calendar provider. I never receive them.

What the app stores, and where

Everything the app stores stays on your phone, in storage only the app can read:

  • Your account details: the calendar server, your user name (for iCloud, your Apple ID) and your password. For iCloud this is an app-specific password, not your Apple ID password. The password is encrypted with a key held in your phone's Android Keystore.
  • A copy of your calendars and events, so the app works quickly and offline.
  • Your settings, such as which calendars to show, and whether you own Orchard Pro.

None of this is included in Android's cloud backup or in phone-to-phone transfers.

Where the app sends data

  • Your calendar provider. To show and change your calendars, the app connects directly to the server you sign in to: Apple's iCloud calendar server, or another CalDAV server you enter. It sends your user name and password to that server to sign in, and sends the events you create or edit. These connections are encrypted (HTTPS). That provider's own privacy policy applies to the data it holds, such as Apple's privacy policy for iCloud.
  • Google Play, if you buy Orchard Pro. The purchase is made and processed by Google Play; I do not receive your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies.

There is no Orchard server. I do not collect, receive, sell or share your calendar data, your password or any other personal information. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code.

Deleting your data

Orchard Calendar keeps your data only on your phone, so you delete it yourself, at any time, without asking anyone:

  1. One account: in Orchard Calendar, open Calendars, then the settings gear, then Accounts, choose the account and tap Remove Account. Its password and cached calendars are deleted from your phone straight away.
  2. Everything: uninstall Orchard Calendar. Android deletes every password, cached calendar and setting it stored.

Nothing is kept afterwards and there is no retention period: there is no Orchard server, so the developer holds no copy to delete. Your events themselves live with your calendar provider (for example iCloud) and are not affected; delete those with your provider. A purchase of Orchard Pro is recorded by Google Play, under Google's privacy policy.

Children

Orchard Calendar is not directed at children under 13.

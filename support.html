Signing in to iCloud

iCloud does not accept your Apple ID password from apps like this one. It needs an app-specific password, which takes a minute to create:

  1. Go to appleid.apple.com and sign in.
  2. Open Sign-In and Security, then App-Specific Passwords.
  3. Create one, name it "Orchard Calendar", and copy the password it shows.
  4. In Orchard Calendar, enter your Apple ID and paste that password.

Your Apple ID needs two-factor authentication turned on to create app-specific passwords.

Common questions

"That Apple ID or app-specific password wasn't accepted." Check that you used an app-specific password, not your normal Apple ID password. If you revoked it at appleid.apple.com, create a new one and sign in again.

A new event isn't showing on my iPhone yet. Events reach iCloud straight away; your other devices show them on their next refresh. Check you saved it to a calendar that your iPhone also shows.

How often does it sync? In the background roughly every 15, 30 or 60 minutes (Settings, Sync Interval; Android may stretch this to save battery), and whenever you tap Sync Now.

I bought Orchard Pro on another phone. Sign in to Google Play with the same Google account and tap Settings, Restore Purchase.

Can I use a calendar that isn't iCloud? Yes: on the sign-in screen choose "Use another CalDAV server", or in Settings choose Add Account, Other CalDAV Server.
